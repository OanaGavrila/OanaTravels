Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how OanaTravels ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website oanatravels.com ("Website"), purchase our digital products, or subscribe to our newsletter.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Email address: collected at checkout for order delivery and optionally at newsletter subscription
  • Payment information: processed entirely by our payment providers (PayPal, Revolut). We do not store credit card numbers, bank details, or any payment credentials on our servers
  • Usage data: anonymous analytics (page views, referrer) may be collected to improve our Website

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address solely for:

  • Delivering your purchased digital products (maps, guides, packs)
  • Sending order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Sending newsletter updates about new destinations and offers (only if you subscribed)
  • Responding to your support requests

We will never sell, rent, or trade your email address to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • PayPal: payment processing (PayPal Privacy Policy)
  • Revolut: payment processing (Revolut Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: font delivery (no personal data collected)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

5. Cookies

Our Website uses minimal cookies necessary for functionality (e.g., shopping cart session). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time (via the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us)
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data

8. Data Retention

We retain your email address only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Order records are kept for accounting and support purposes. Newsletter subscriptions are maintained until you unsubscribe.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Website is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at hi@oanatravels.com.
